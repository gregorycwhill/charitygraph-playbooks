# CharityGraph Playbook Contract

**Status:** Product-specific contract, draft v0.1

**Scope:** Governed analytical-method definitions and their portable use with CharityGraph; no hosted inference or production catalogue is defined here.

## 1. Product boundary

Playbooks publishes open, reusable analytical methods for applying governed CharityGraph Data with a general-purpose AI system selected by the user. It is a separate product from Builder, Data and Viewer. Builder constructs knowledge; Data publishes it; Viewer supports inspection and contextual entry points; Playbooks provides methods.

Shared CharityGraph product principles, commitments, evidence rules, privacy rules, contestability rules and brand/reuse policy govern this contract. A future Playbooks-specific contract may refine those rules but may not override them.

## 2. Playbook Definition

A **Playbook Definition** is a governed, stably identified and versioned analytical method. A definition may be Official or Community and may be proposed, active, deprecated or superseded. It describes an analytical job and how a user-selected AI should use CharityGraph material without claiming that downstream analysis is CharityGraph knowledge.

The minimal machine contract is represented by `schema/playbook.schema.json` and supports:

- `schema_version`, `id`, `version`, `title` and `description`;
- `governance_status` and `lifecycle_status`;
- contributors and attribution;
- applicable subject grains;
- typed parameters and their public/private visibility;
- CharityGraph context/data requirements and optional external-evidence requirements;
- model/tool capability requirements without requiring a provider;
- instruction template and output expectations;
- limitations and non-inference rules;
- inherited `base_policy_version`;
- feedback routes;
- licence; and
- optional supersession/deprecation lineage.

Individual Playbooks may add stricter requirements, narrower scope or additional review. They must not weaken the Base Policy.

## 3. Invocation

An **Invocation** is a parameterised instance of one Playbook version. It records, where disclosure is appropriate:

- the Playbook identifier and version;
- concrete parameter values;
- CharityGraph Data release or snapshot identity;
- relevant subject, program or service references;
- optional packaged public context; and
- generation metadata sufficient for reproducibility.

An invocation may be generated by Viewer or another client. It belongs to the user's analysis context, is not itself canonical CharityGraph knowledge, and need not be published. User strategic parameters may be private.

## 4. External-model output

External-model output is downstream analysis produced in the user's chosen AI environment. It is not an official CharityGraph release, assertion, Data correction, Playbook version or evidence merely because an official Playbook produced it. The user or downstream publisher is responsible for any additional evidence, claims and decisions in that output.

## 5. Parameter design

Use known context to eliminate parameters before asking the user for them. The machine contract may be typed, but user-facing questions should be plain-language.

Parameters may include CharityGraph subject/program/service references, geography, decision context, user-provided constraints and external-data references. Public reproducibility metadata must be distinguished from private user inputs. Playbook definitions must not require collection of private strategy information.

## 6. Retrieval and context modes

An invocation may:

1. **retrieve** canonical CharityGraph representations by URL; or
2. **package context** with relevant public CharityGraph material when live retrieval is unreliable.

The consuming model must disclose inability to retrieve or receive the intended CharityGraph material and must not silently substitute generic model knowledge while claiming CharityGraph use.

## 7. Versioning and corrigibility

Playbook versions remain resolvable and attributable. An analysis should be able to record the Playbook version, CharityGraph Data release or snapshot and disclosed parameter values. Definitions are corrigible through governed review, correction and supersession; immutable released versions are not rewritten.

## 8. Feedback boundary

Keep these pathways distinct:

1. a CharityGraph Data/content error;
2. a Playbook method/design problem; and
3. an external execution, model or retrieval problem.

This contract does not define a production catalogue, a provider integration, a Viewer UI or a hosted inference service.
