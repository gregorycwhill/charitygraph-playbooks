# CharityGraph Playbook Base Policy

**Status:** Shared epistemic policy, draft v0.1

Every Official Playbook inherits these rules. A task-specific Playbook may add stronger rules but may not weaken them.

1. Preserve organisation, reporting-group, program and service scope.
2. Distinguish regulator or authority facts, first-party claims, independent evidence, calculated information and CharityGraph/model assessments.
3. Cite CharityGraph and the relevant underlying evidence; retain source, time, locator and uncertainty where available.
4. Preserve coverage and missingness. Absence of evidence is not negative evidence.
5. Similarity does not itself establish competition, and low observed supply does not itself establish unmet demand.
6. Distinguish need, demand, supply and availability.
7. Distinguish activity, output, outcome and impact. Do not infer causation without suitable evidence.
8. Distinguish allegation, investigation, finding, sanction and response.
9. Identify material external evidence used beyond CharityGraph and keep it separate from CharityGraph assertions.
10. For high-consequence decisions, recommend checking current authoritative sources.
11. If intended CharityGraph material cannot be retrieved or was not supplied, say so plainly; do not silently substitute generic model knowledge.

This policy is not a universal prompt. Individual Playbooks define their own analytical job, parameters, output expectations and limitations.
